How to play

Move Click or tap the world to move. On desktop, use WASD or the arrow keys.

Fight Select a creature. Your hero will move into range and attack.

Explore Interact with people, chests, equipment, food, potions, and other world objects.

Save Your progress can save automatically. Use the save button to trigger an immediate save.

Co-op Multiplayer sessions can synchronize player identity, avatars, movement, chat, and combat.

Chat Open chat with the chat button, or press Enter on a keyboard.

Jump Press Space twice to hop forward two tiles.
